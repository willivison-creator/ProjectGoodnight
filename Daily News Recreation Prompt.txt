Daily News Recreation Prompt
Create a 60–90 second country-pop broadcast theme in a bright major key at approximately 115 BPM. Use acoustic guitar, clean electric guitar, bass, live drums, subtle keyboards, and polished radio production. Feature a warm, friendly male lead vocalist with clear diction and a conversational delivery. Add tasteful multi-part backing harmonies during the chorus only. Keep the arrangement concise with a short intro, one verse, one memorable chorus, and a clean ending. The lyrics should summarize today's local news in an upbeat, community-focused style, mentioning weather, local events, traffic, sports, and one positive community story. Avoid sensationalism. Write completely new lyrics each day while maintaining an optimistic, welcoming tone suitable for a morning radio broadcast. The chorus should be catchy, easy to remember, and reinforce staying informed about the local community. Mix the song with modern commercial radio polish, centered vocals, stereo acoustic guitars, moderate compression, light reverb, and a bright, warm overall sound.
